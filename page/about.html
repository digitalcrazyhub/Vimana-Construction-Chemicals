Our Story

A Decade of Building Trust

From a small waterproofing crew to a trusted name in construction chemicals — every milestone has been built on quality and commitment.

  1. 2014

    The Beginning

    Founded with a mission to solve chronic water damage across Indian rooftops and basements.

  2. 2017

    Growth Journey

    Expanded to commercial and industrial projects, adding certified applicators and lab-tested products.

  3. 2020

    Major Achievements

    Crossed 500+ successful sites and launched our proprietary chemical product line.

  4. 2024

    Today

    A leading regional waterproofing partner with 1000+ completed structures and a nationwide vision.

  5. 2030

    Future Goals

    Sustainable chemistry, IoT-enabled inspections and pan-India expertise for the next generation of structures.
